Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Fast, private, and made for real conversations. Open the app and you're one tap from talking to someone new.

Anonymous text chat

Instant one-to-one text chat with a random stranger. No names, no profiles, no chat history saved.

Voice calls

Switch from text to a clear voice call whenever both of you are up for it.

Video calls

Face-to-face video with strangers, powered by fast peer-to-peer WebRTC connections.

No signup, ever

No email, no phone number, no password. Open the app, confirm you're 18+, and start meeting people.

Instant matching

Novis finds someone for you in a few seconds. Not feeling the conversation? Tap Next and move on.

Safety built in

Report and block in one tap. You decide who you talk to, and for how long.

How it works

Talking in under 10 seconds

No forms, no feeds, no followers — just conversations with real people.

1

Open Novis

Download the app (or open the web app) and confirm you're 18 or older. That's the only gate.

2

Pick text, voice or video

Choose how you want to connect and Novis instantly finds a stranger who's online right now.

3

Chat — or tap Next

Great conversation? Keep going. Not for you? End or skip to the next person, no hard feelings.

Safety first

Built to keep you safe

Novis is strictly for adults, and you stay in charge of every conversation.

18+ only

Novis is an adults-only app. An age confirmation is required before your first match.

Report & block

Bad behaviour? Report or block in one tap — you'll never be matched with that person again.

Stay anonymous

Keep personal details to yourself: your real name, address, phone number, school and social handles.

Leave anytime

You can end any chat with one tap. Once it ends, it's gone — nothing is kept.
