Features

How it works

Talking in under 10 seconds

No forms. No feeds. No followers. Just real conversations with real people.

1

Open Novis

Download the app (or open the web app) and confirm you're 18 or older. That's the only gate.

2

Pick text, voice or video

Choose how you want to connect and Novis instantly finds a stranger who's online right now.

3

Chat — or tap Next

Great conversation? Keep going. Not for you? End or skip to the next person, no hard feelings.

Safety first

Anonymous doesn't mean unprotected

Novis is strictly for adults, and you stay in charge of every conversation.

18+ only

Novis is an adults-only app. An age confirmation is required before your first match.

Report & block

Bad behaviour? Report or block in one tap — you'll never be matched with that person again.

Stay anonymous

Never share personal details — your real name, address, phone number, school or social handles.

Leave anytime

Every chat is one tap from over. When a conversation ends, it's gone — nothing is kept.
